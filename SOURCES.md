# Source and attribution register

Checked 10 November 2026. This is a record of what this edition contains. It is not a license from any manufacturer, and it is not legal advice. A lawyer has not reviewed it.

## Original

Text, flowcharts, maintenance wording, and the application code written for RivetCare. Copyright © 2026 Charles Moe. See LICENSE and OWNERSHIP.md.

A buyer may use the app for their own personal use. A buyer may not copy, resell, or republish it.

The icon is an original panel on a navy field. It is not an Airstream photograph or logo.

The 22 procedures name the sheet a number came from. The sentences are this book's words. They are not a pasted manual page. The safety numbers were not rewritten into different numbers.

## Fonts

Barlow, Source Serif 4, and Outfit ship in `public/fonts` under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The license text is `OFL-Barlow.txt`, `OFL-SourceSerif4.txt`, and `OFL-Outfit.txt`. Those licenses allow the fonts to ship inside software that is sold. The fonts are not sold by themselves.

## Shop distance

`src/data/zips.json` is the US Census Bureau 2024 Gazetteer file of ZIP Code Tabulation Area internal points. That is public-domain government work. It is not the earlier file, which had no license on record. Shop names, streets, phones, and hours stay. Hours notes that quoted another website were removed.

## Not original

- Airstream, Truma, Dometic, Atwood, Suburban, Alde, Battle Born, Dexter, Victron, Norcold, Garnet, Lippert, Progressive Dynamics, SHURflo, Goodyear, GE, Fogatti, and Fantastic Fan are trademarks of their owners. The app names the equipment. It does not use their logos.
- Factory manuals and parts books are not stored in the app. A link opens Airstream’s site.
- Fault codes, torque, pressure, and end-play figures are named only where a step says which sheet they came from. If a step does not name a source, it is not a factory specification.
- This edition does not store a tire maker's load rows. The placard is the pressure. The tire size stays on the coach card.
- Owner-reported steps are labeled in the app. They are not factory procedures.
- Shop names and addresses were taken from public listings. A listing is not permission to republish a dealer’s own site, and it is not a visit.

## Not in this edition

Diagrams, maker troubleshooting charts, and the six factory circuit lists are not shipped. The Figures shelf is a short note that points at the written checks and the manual. The picture files are not in `public/figures`.

## What is not in the app

The factory parts catalog is not in the app, and it is not in the public project zip. Factory parts-book extracts, appliance-code archives, and development screenshots stay in the workspace only. `scripts/pack-source.mjs` leaves `attachments/`, `screenshots/`, and `.grok/` out.

## Network

The app loads from its own address. It does not send the log, the VIN, or photos with that load. A manual link goes to Airstream. A recall check goes to NHTSA. A map link opens the map app. There is no analytics request.

## Before a sale

The grant in LICENSE is a paid personal license. It is not a clearance. A lawyer still has to read the remaining text, the cited numbers, and the shop list before the book is sold.
