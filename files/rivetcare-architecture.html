On the phone

localStorage
  • rivetcare-v1 trailer, miles, marks, log, notes
  • rivetcare-weights scale tickets
  • rivetcare-level and rivetcare-level-zero
  • rivetcare-service last shop search
  • rivetcare-pass sale code, kept when records are deleted
IndexedDB
  • rivetcare-files photos and PDFs
  • rivetcare-books pages saved for offline
  • rivetcare-undo the book before a restore

Shared controls

All in src/components/ui.tsx. Screens compose them. Nothing subclasses them.

Sheet, Card, Kicker, PageTitle, Pill, Chip, DarkModeSwitch, Notice, Btn.

One sheet frame: trailer picker, disclaimer, sale-code gate, backup, and restore.

What can leave the phone

  • A shop lookup, when a ZIP is used.
  • A manual link, which opens Airstream's site.
  • The NHTSA recalls button. The VIN is copied, not sent by the app.
  • A backup file the owner shares or saves. It is not uploaded to an account.
